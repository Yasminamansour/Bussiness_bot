The Paper Lantern Library - Business Summary
(Fictional business created for EECE 503P/798S - Agentic Systems)

The Paper Lantern Library is a small community library on the second floor of 14 Makdessi Street in Hamra, Beirut. Founded in 2019 by librarian Rania Haddad, its mission is to make reading and creativity accessible to everyone by combining a lending library, a quiet study space, and an art studio under one roof. The library holds around 8,000 books in Arabic, English, and French, and is open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. It is closed on Sundays, on public holidays, and during the winter break (December 20 to January 4).

Memberships cost $5/month for students (with a valid student ID), $8/month for adults, and $15/month for families of up to four people. A day pass costs $2. Members can borrow up to 4 books at a time for 3 weeks, with one renewal allowed. Late returns cost $0.25 per book per day. Members get free use of the reading room, which has Wi-Fi and backup generator power, so it stays open during electricity cuts. Every Saturday at 11:00 AM there is a free story time for children in the Kids' Corner.

The library's signature program is the Lantern Sketch Sessions: drawing classes held every three weeks on Saturdays from 3:00 PM to 5:30 PM, for teens and adults aged 14 and up. Each session has a theme and is taught by illustrator Karim Nassar. Classes are limited to 15 participants and cost $10 for members or $15 for non-members, with all materials included (sketchbook, graphite pencils, charcoal, and erasers). Children aged 7 to 12 can join the Little Sketchers session on the same Saturdays from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM ($6 for members, $8 for non-members, maximum 10 children). Upcoming sessions are: October 24, 2026 (Urban Sketching: Beirut Balconies), November 14, 2026 (Portrait Basics), December 5, 2026 (Still Life: Light and Shadow), and January 16, 2027 (Ink and Wash). Registration closes the Thursday before each session. Cancellations made at least 48 hours in advance receive a full refund; later cancellations receive a credit toward a future session. When a class is full, students can join a waitlist.

What makes The Paper Lantern Library unique is that it is a library and an art studio in one: an affordable, quiet, always-powered space where people can read, study, and learn to draw, with every art material included in the class price. The team includes Rania Haddad (founder and head librarian), Karim Nassar (drawing instructor), Maya Khoury (children's programs coordinator), and Joe Saliba (memberships and front desk). Visitors can register for classes or ask questions in person at the front desk or by email at hello@paperlanternlibrary.example.
